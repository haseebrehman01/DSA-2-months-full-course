Notation    Symbol    Case            Also Called
Big-O       O         Worst Case      Upper Bound
Theta       θ         Average Case    Tight Bound
Omega       Ω         Best Case       Lower Bound



Why Upper Bound and Lower Bound?

Upper Bound (O) → algorithm will never be slower than this. this is the maximum.
Lower Bound (Ω) → algorithm will never be faster than this. this is the minimum.
Tight Bound (θ) → sits right in the middle. average behavior.


Most Important Point:
In DSA and interviews, when someone says "what is the time complexity" they almost always mean Big-O (worst case). Theta and Omega are less commonly asked but good to know.
